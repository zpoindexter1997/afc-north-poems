Oh, AFC North, your battles are fierce,
But this week’s defeats have cut deep, and they pierce.
We marched to the Ravens, pride in our chest,
But Saturday's clash put us to the test.
Russell Wilson threw like a man possessed,
But in crucial moments, he left us distressed.
A fumble here, a turnover there,
Our hopes dissolved into Baltimore’s air.
Pickens was absent, a glaring void,
Calvin Austin stepped up, a spark deployed.
Yet his strides alone couldn’t turn the tide,
And in the end, we were cast aside.
The Bengals and Browns, a woeful affair,
A clash of despair left fans in despair.
DTR stumbled, the offense stood still,
While the Bengals scraped by, bending their will.
Jameis benched, the Browns in dismay,
The Dawg Pound silenced on this dreary day.
The Bengals cling to their playoff dream,
While Cleveland falters, unraveling at the seam.
Now Christmas looms, the Chiefs await,
A formidable foe, a daunting fate.
The Steelers must rise, with grit and might,
To give their fans a holiday fight.
Oh, AFC North, you twist and you turn,
Your fiery rivalries make passions burn.
But through the losses, our faith remains,
For Steelers’ pride flows in our veins.
