Browns @ Bears — Reality in Chicago
Cleveland traveled cold to Soldier’s field,
But Shadeur’s heart was the only shield.
Chicago struck early, then never slowed,
Every Browns mistake loudly showed.
31-3, the outcome sealed,
A lost-season team fully revealed.

Ravens @ Bengals — No Fun at All
Just weeks ago, Cincy stood tall,
This time? Nothing worked at all.
Burrow forced throws, the picks stacked high,
While Ravens points kept drifting by.
After saying football wasn’t “fun” to play,
24-0 proved he felt that way.

Dolphins @ Steelers — Cold Weather, Same Story
Miami came north with division weight,
Pittsburgh struck fast, no time to wait.
Rodgers dealt darts, the run game chipped,
28-3 before the fourth even tipped.
A late push failed, the streak lives on,
28-15 — MNF dominance strong.
