We waited long into the night,
The moon hung low, the stars shone bright,
But Steelers fell, their fight undone,
Cowboys smiled as they had won.
The clock struck one, and hearts sank deep,
As fans we watched, forsaking sleep.
At home they lost, in black and gold,
The fire dimmed, the air turned cold.
Yet further south, the Ravens soared,
With MVPs both sides adored.
A clash of titans, offense loud,
The fans in awe, the city proud.
But for the Bengals, hope turns thin,
At 1-4, it's hard to win.
Though strong they played, with heart and might,
Their playoff dreams fade out of sight.
The Browns, oh Browns, how low they’ve sunk,
With Watson’s will a broken trunk.
The fans looked on, despairing deep,
As Cleveland’s hope begins to weep.
Now Russell whispers in the air,
He wants to be our QB’s heir.
Fields has stumbled, lost his spark,
And Wilson lurks to make his mark.
But still, we wait for Sunday’s light,
For answers clear, for wrongs made right.
The peril looms, the questions soar,
In seven days, we’ll know much more.
