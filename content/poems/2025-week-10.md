Browns @ Jets — The Magic Runs Dry
Cleveland flew out to face New York skies,
But Gabriel’s spark was just disguise.
No rhythm found, no true attack,
The Jets pushed first and never looked back.
27-20, hope fading thin—
At 2-7, change might begin.

Ravens @ Vikings — Purple on Purple Crime
Baltimore marched into Minnesota's hue,
White and purple, a color muddle crew.
But Lamar shined bright, impossible to miss,
Every drive felt like controlled bliss.
27-19, Ravens climb alive—
At 4-5, they start to thrive.

Steelers @ Chargers — Primetime Pain
Sunday night lights washed in regret,
Rodgers struggled, the worst one yet.
Pressure collapsing, no space to breathe,
The offense cracked, defense couldn’t weave.
25-10, the stumble hits sore—
At 5-4, we lead… but unsure.

Bengals — Bye Week Silence
No game played, no score to show,
But jokes flew fast — we all know.
“For once,” they said, “no 30 allowed.”
A quiet week, no cheering crowd.
Still 3-6, watching the rest,
Wondering if better days manifest.

Standings after Week 10:
Steelers 5-4 (still leading, but barely). Ravens 4-5, Bengals 3-6, Browns 2-7
Division Narrative:
Pittsburgh’s grip weakens — leadership without cushion.
Baltimore is rising again, momentum solid and real.
Cincinnati watches from the shadows, hoping the break resets the fire.
Cleveland sinks deeper, searching for the next quarterback lifeline.
