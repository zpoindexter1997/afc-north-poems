Steelers Stumble in Seattle
Metcalf scored once in a revenge-fueled fight,
But the Steelers’ defense looked far from right.
Seattle pressed on, our spark grew dim,
A rookie’s mistake sealed hopes so slim.
31-17, we fall to one and one,
Early season work is far from done.

Backup Browning’s Bite
Burrow went down, the crowd held tight,
Browning stepped in to guide the night.
The Jags kept pressing, but Cincy stood tall,
Defense and grit decided it all.
31-27, Bengals keep their flow,
At 2-0, they’re stealing the show.

Baltimore’s Bold Rebuttal
The Browns talked big, but the field said no,
Lamar lit flames, put on a show.
Henry was quiet, yet Ravens soared,
Flacco returned, but still got floored.
41-17, the message was sent,
Baltimore’s back with strong intent.

Standings after Week 2:
Bengals 2-0, Steelers 1-1, Ravens 1-1, Browns 0-2.
Cincy claims the crown, though Burrow’s health looms large.
Baltimore shows resilience, reminding all they’re contenders.
Pittsburgh struggles with discipline, flashes hope but flaws remain.
And Cleveland—0-2 and talking loud, but walking nowhere.
