Jets @ Bengals — The Collapse at Home
The Jets came to Cincy, winless and worn,
But the Bengals led strong, their confidence born.
Flacco threw strikes, Chase danced in space,
38-24 felt like a safe place.
But Fields lit fire, the comeback was fate—
39-38, heartbreak at 3-5 state.

Patriots @ Browns — Back to Reality
New England rolled into Cleveland town,
Drake Maye heating up, laying bodies down.
Judkins was quiet, Gabriel stalled,
The Browns’ defense broke, no stops installed.
32-13, dreams hit the floor—
At 2-6 now, hope’s out the door.

Bears @ Ravens — Snoop’s Surprise
Chicago arrived expecting a break,
But Huntley got the nod, no time to shake.
He ran like Lamar, kept drives alive,
The Bears never matched that Baltimore drive.
30-16, the Ravens revive—
At 2-5, they’re still in the strive.

Packers @ Steelers — The Second Half Slip
Green Bay came to Pittsburgh, Rodgers’ old flame,
First half was ours—field goals won the claim.
16-7 lead, the crowd felt right,
But the Packers struck back with a second-half bite.
25-35, we drop to 4-3,
Still atop the North, but barely free.

Standings after Week 8:
Steelers 4-3, Bengals 3-5, Ravens 2-5, Browns 2-6
Division Narrative:
The Steelers still lead, but they’re hanging on by threads.
The Bengals are fireworks one minute and ashes the next.
The Ravens cling to life thanks to Huntley’s stepping up.
The Browns slide fast, the season slipping beyond reach.
