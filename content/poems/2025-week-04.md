Steelers Send a Message in Ireland
Across the sea, the fight began,
DK’s eighty-yard strike thrilled every fan.
The defense feasted, turnovers came,
Forced fumbles, picks, they set the flame.
Late mistakes nearly spoiled the run,
24-21, but the job got done.

Cleveland Crashes Back to Earth
A hot start teased a shocking trend,
Could the Browns topple giants again?
But the Lions roared, no mercy shown,
34-10, the truth was known.
At 1-3, the call grows clear,
Rookie Gabriel’s chance may soon appear.

Ravens Crumble in Kansas City
Injuries stacked, the roster thinned,
Even Lamar’s hamstring pinned.
Henry was bottled, no spark in sight,
The Chiefs rolled on in primetime light.
37-20, the damage was real,
At 1-3, the wounds won’t heal.

Bengals’ Mile High Misery
No picks from Browning, but that’s no cheer,
The yards were scarce, the end too near.
Broncos ran wild, five-thirty gained,
Cincy looked lost, momentum drained.
3-28, the spiral’s begun,
At 2-2, the doubts weigh a ton.

Standings after Week 4:
Steelers 3-1, Bengals 2-2, Browns 1-3, Ravens 1-3.
Division Narrative:
Pittsburgh rises above the chaos, powered by defense and deep strikes.
Cincy sinks into a funk, Browning unable to spark the offense.
Cleveland’s brief flame flickers, now turning toward their rookie for hope.
Baltimore battles injuries, their season at risk of slipping away early.
