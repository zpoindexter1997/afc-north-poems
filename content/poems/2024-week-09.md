During our Bye, we took a seat,
Watched our rivals rise or face defeat.
The Ravens soared, fierce and strong,
Crushing the Broncos, proving they belong.

Lamar’s crew put on a show,
A warning shot for all to know.
Scary and swift, they left no doubt,
Baltimore’s roar was loud and stout.

The Bengals roared in the jungle’s den,
Taming the Raiders, leaving them thin.
Burrow’s arm, like a cannon blaze,
Five touchdowns, an electrifying phase.

Chase Brown dashed with nimble might,
Bengal stripes gleamed, eyes alight.
Raiders faltered, a dismal sight,
But Cincinnati’s win was pure delight.

Alas, the Browns with Winston’s hand,
Faced the Chargers, a tough demand.
Three picks thrown, a costly game,
Yet Winston’s faith burned just the same.

Momentum stalled, but hope’s not gone,
In Cleveland’s heart, the fight goes on.
The Dawg Pound waits, restless and proud,
Seeking redemption, fierce and loud.

Now we Steelers rise from our rest,
Prepared to put our skills to test.
The Commanders wait with Daniels new,
A rookie spark, a challenge true.

Will we silence his bold debut,
And wave our Terrible Towels through?
From the ashes of the bye, we rise,
Steel in our blood, fire in our eyes.

The North grows fierce, the battle’s nigh,
But Steeler grit will not deny.
Onward, black and gold we ride,
To claim our place, with pride on side.

P.S. Don’t be 22 on the Jags. Don’t let a grown man hurdle you while running backwards.
