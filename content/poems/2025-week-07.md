The Icy Hot Bowl
Old men dueling under the lights,
Rodgers and Flacco, two aging knights.
Pittsburgh struck first, a 10-0 start,
But Chase tore holes through the Steelers’ heart.
A 79-yard prayer fell shy of grace,
33-31 — defeat’s cold embrace.

Cleveland’s Rookie Rampage
The Dolphins sank in Cleveland’s storm,
That defense held in perfect form.
Gabriel steady, but Judkins shined,
Three touchdowns scored, the game defined.
31-6, the Browns awake,
At 2-5, they rise in stake.

Ravens’ Bye Week Blues
No game to play, no ground to gain,
The Ravens rest, but still feel pain.
As Cleveland climbs, their fall’s in view,
At 1-5, they’re slipping through.
Baltimore’s break brings no delight,
They’re dead last now — no purple might.
