# AFC North Recap Week 5 - 10/12/2026
*"The North Never Stays Quiet"*

### Colts @ Steelers — The Steel City Strikes Back

After Cleveland's defeat, we had something to prove,  
And Pittsburgh came roaring with a whole different groove.  
Three straight hundred-yard weeks, Warren ran wild,  
Two touchdowns to show why this man's so highly styled.  
Rodgers defied Father Time, DK answered the call,  
While injuries couldn't stop our offense at all.  
Taylor was bottled, Jones struggled to score,  
**34-22 — Pittsburgh's back in the war.**

---

### Bengals @ Dolphins — A Win Too Close for Comfort

With Higgins and Chase back, the Bengals found hope,  
But against lowly Miami, they struggled to cope.  
Ja'Marr made a catch that had jaws on the floor,  
Reminding the league what his greatness is for.  
The Dolphins kept fighting, refusing to fade,  
And Cincy survived by the plays that they made.  
A win's still a win, though the doubts aren't gone,  
**31-24 — the Bengals keep marching on.**

---

### Browns @ Jets — Back Down to Earth

Cleveland came rolling with three wins in a row,  
But New York had plans to put an end to the show.  
Watson looked lost, the offense ran dry,  
Two struggling teams with no sparks flying high.  
The Jets' defense feasted, their pressure was real,  
While Garrett Wilson gave Cleveland more pain to feel.  
Their division lead vanished, the hot streak was through,  
**17-12 — the Browns fall back to 3-2.**

---

### Ravens @ Falcons — Snoop Saves the Night

With Lamar sidelined, Atlanta smelled blood,  
But Snoop took the reins and stepped into the flood.  
Henry kept pounding, while Flowers took flight,  
Yet Tua and the Falcons refused to lose the fight.  
Overtime arrived, then Tua threw one away,  
And Baltimore seized their chance to end the day.  
Without their MVP, the Ravens still thrive,  
**30-23 — Baltimore rises to 4-1 alive.**

---

**Standings after Week 5:** Ravens **4-1** | Steelers **3-2** | Bengals **3-2** | Browns **3-2**

**Division Narrative:**

Baltimore stands alone atop the North, proving that even without Lamar Jackson, Henry, Flowers, and Huntley can keep this machine rolling.

Pittsburgh silences the doubters with its most complete offensive showing yet, as Warren breaks records and Rodgers proves that age is just a number.

Cincinnati survives a scare against one of the league's weakest teams, but Chase's brilliance and the return of their receiving weapons keep them dangerous.

Cleveland's Cinderella run takes a nasty hit, with Watson regressing and the Browns surrendering their share of the division lead.

**Five weeks down: The Ravens rule without their king, the Steelers rise from the ashes, the Bengals survive by inches, and Cleveland remembers what losing feels like.**
