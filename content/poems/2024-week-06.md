In the AFC North, the tide starts to rise,
Steelers' reign is clear in every set of eyes.
Dominating Raiders, both offense and D,
Justin Fields was flying, so wild and free.
TJ Watt, a beast on the prowl,
Striking fear with each relentless growl.
Mike Tomlin, smiling, all game long,
The Steelers' fight is mighty and strong.
Ravens, too, are making their claim,
Jayden Daniels felt the heat of the game.
But Lamar’s still ruling with grace and might,
Keeping their 4-2 record tight.
The Bengals, though stumbling, found their way,
Joe Burrow running, no room to play.
Against Danny Dimes, they kept it close,
Staying in the hunt, still chasing the most.
The Browns, though battered, put up a fight,
Against the Eagles, they showed some light.
But 1-5 in a tough, tight race,
Playoff dreams seem lost in space.
Still, hope might rise with a change at the helm,
Jameis Winston could steady the realm.
Fans call for Watson’s time to end,
Maybe a new chapter, just around the bend.
The North is fierce, a battlefield untamed,
But the Steelers stand tall, never ashamed.
