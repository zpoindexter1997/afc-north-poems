Ravens @ Packers — The King Carries On
No Lamar, no Love — just chaos to start,
But Derrick Henry tore Green Bay apart.
Two hundred sixteen and four to the house,
The man ran angry, unstoppable, groused.
41-24, the Ravens survive,
Playoff hopes live — barely alive.

Steelers @ Browns — A Collapse for the Ages
No DK to spark, no rhythm to find,
A must-win moment, and we went blind.
Cleveland struck first, then twisted the knife,
While our offense showed zero life.
6-13 loss, the season on edge,
One game left now — walk or ledge.

Cardinals @ Bengals — Too Little, Too Late
Cincy came out with nothing to lose,
Burrow dealt darts, picked apart the blues.
Touchdowns flowed like it mattered again,
But the season’s fate was already penned.
37-14, a hollow delight,
Just echoes of what could have been right.
