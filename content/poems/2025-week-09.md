Ravens @ Dolphins — Lamar’s Return
Baltimore flew into Miami heat,
Lamar came back with MVP feet.
Darts through the air, defenders spun,
The Dolphins wilted — this one was done.
28-6, a statement clear,
At 3-5, the Ravens reappear.

Colts @ Steelers — Defense Awakens
Indy rolled in looking top-tier strong,
But our defense said they were wrong.
Six turnovers forced, the crowd went wild,
Big TEs bullied, the offense styled.
27-20, the standard alive,
At 5-3, we continue to thrive. 🖤💛

Bears @ Bengals — The Shootout Heartbreaker
Cincy and Chicago in chaos mode,
Touchdown for touchdown, the scoreboard glowed.
An onside kick? They got it, insane—
Flacco’s fire poured through every vein.
But the Bears struck last, 47-42 —
At 3-6, what more can Cincy do?

Browns — Bye Week Breathing
Cleveland sat out, no highs or cries,
No hope gained, but no new goodbyes.
Still 2-6, watching the storm,
Waiting to see who changes form.

Standings after Week 9:
Steelers 5-3, Ravens 3-5, Bengals 3-6, Browns 2-6
Division Narrative:
Pittsburgh reclaims identity — defense dictating destiny.
Baltimore resurfaces with Lamar’s spark and belief restored.
Cincinnati plays with heart, but heartbreak keeps tightening the vise.
Cleveland watches from the bye — but time isn’t slowing for anyone.
