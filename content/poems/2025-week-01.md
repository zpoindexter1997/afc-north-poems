Revenge was sweet, the stage set right,
Fields ran twice, Jets fans took flight.
But Rodgers in black and gold took command,
Four touchdowns tossed with a veteran’s hand.
Steelers’ run D cracked, the weak point they showed,
Yet 34-32, the victory road.
A shaky defense, but a win to see,
Pittsburgh smiles at 1-0, with glee.

Battle of Stalled Engines
In Cleveland and Cincy, the sparks ran thin,
Both offenses struggled, no rhythm within.
Myles Garrett hunted, Burrow hit the ground,
Yet Bengals’ defense kept points to the bound.
Flacco found flashes, but not quite enough,
The trenches defined this one as tough.
A 17-16 grind, Bengals snuck through,
1-0 start, with division dreams in view.

Ravens’ Roar, Bills’ Bite
Henry was pounding, defenders fell low,
Zay Flowers soared, the Ravens did glow.
“MVP” chants thundered for Lamar’s might,
Baltimore seemed ready to steal the night.
But Allen struck back with magic to spare,
Bills clawed their way in a comeback rare.
Henry’s late fumble cracked open the door,
A field goal sealed it, 41-40, the score.

Week 1 / 9-4-25 / Steelers 1-0, Bengals 1-0, Ravens 0-1, Browns 0-1.
The war for the North has only begun.
