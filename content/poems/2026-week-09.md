# AFC North Recap Week 9 - 11/8/2026
*"The Jungle Roars While the North Falls Apart"*

## Browns @ Saints — A Spark That Couldn't Save Cleveland

With Watson concussed, young Shedeur took the stage,  
Two scores, no picks, nearly three hundred on the page.  
He showed some promise, gave Cleveland a light,  
But drives kept stalling throughout the fight.  
Sixteen apiece, overtime brought the heat,  
The Saints struck first, but the Browns wouldn't accept defeat.  
A touchdown gave hope, but two points slipped away,  
**23-22 — New Orleans spoiled Shedeur's day.**

---

## Jaguars @ Ravens — The Kingdom Has Fallen

Lamar's on the shelf, and Snoop's missing too,  
So Baltimore's rookie had a nightmare to chew.  
No rhythm, no spark, no reason to cheer,  
The Ravens look worse with each passing year.  
Jacksonville rolled in and ran up the score,  
While Baltimore's offense could barely do more.  
From Super Bowl dreams to a season undone,  
**33-10 — the Jaguars had all of the fun.**

---

## Bengals vs Falcons — The Jungle Takes Madrid

Madrid got a show as the offenses took flight,  
Both teams came swinging with plenty of bite.  
But Burrow found Chase, then Higgins once more,  
And Atlanta's poor defense couldn't stop them from scoring.  
Chase Brown found daylight, the Bengals found steam,  
A first-half explosion built Cincinnati's dream.  
The Falcons fought back, but the comeback was through,  
**34-17 — the Jungle came roaring through.**

**Division Narrative:**

Pittsburgh enjoys a much-needed bye week, holding onto first place through its head-to-head advantage over Cincinnati while preparing for the second half of the season.

Cincinnati finally delivers a convincing victory, with Burrow, Chase, and Higgins looking nearly unstoppable as the Bengals pull even with Pittsburgh at 6-2.

Cleveland drops its second straight despite a promising performance from Shedeur Sanders, raising questions about whether its early-season magic has finally run out.

Baltimore's collapse continues with both Lamar Jackson and Tyler Huntley sidelined, leaving a once-feared contender looking completely lost.

**Nine weeks down: Pittsburgh rests upon the throne, Cincinnati roars across the sea, Cleveland's magic starts to fade — and Baltimore begs for mercy.**
