Steelers Keep the Home Streak Alive
Twenty-two straight, the Browns still curse,
At our house, their luck gets worse.
No touchdowns scored, our defense proud,
The crowd roared loud, black and gold and loud.

4-1 strong, first place, no lie.

Ravens Running on Fumes
No Lamar again, the struggle was real,
Rush went down, the wounds won’t heal.
Huntley stepped in, but the spark was gone,
The Rams rolled easy, kept marching on.
17-3, the loss hits deep,

Flacco’s Flash, but Not Enough

A veteran spark, a story retold.
He found the end zone, led a few drives,
But the Packers crushed comeback vibes.
27-18, still short of the call,
At 2-3, the cracks show all.
