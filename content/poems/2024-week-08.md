The AFC North, a battlefield,
Where rival teams will never yield.
The Bengals tried, but lost their way,
The Eagles soared, they couldn’t stay.
From Burrow's roars to whispers low,
Back to the bottom, they did go.
A crushing loss, a painful sight,
But for Steelers fans, a bit of light.
The Ravens clashed with Cleveland’s might,
Winston stepped up under the night.
His passes soared, his spirit high,
He found the end zone in the sky.
The Dawg Pound roared, the Ravens fell,
An upset tale, a tale to tell.
On the final play, the Browns did thrive,
The Ravens shocked, but still alive.
But now it’s Monday, Steelers’ turn,
To watch the Giants crash and burn.
Can Russell lead us back on top?
Can he make the doubters drop?
Odd things have happened, no doubt it’s true,
But here’s to black and gold pushing through.
With hearts of steel and Terrible Towels,
We’ll fight again, through all the howls.
