Steelers @ Bears — A Rudolph Reality Check
Pittsburgh rolled into Chicago cold,
Rudolph starting, courage bold.
But his first pass picked — a brutal tone,
The defense fought hard, but couldn’t atone.
31-28 slipped through our hand,
At 6-5, we still tried to stand.

Bengals @ Patriots — One More for Flacco
Cincy traveled east to Foxborough frost,
No Chase in sight — his temper cost.
Flacco battled strong, refusing to bend,
But 26-20 marked a losing end.
His final ride before Burrow’s claim,
At 3-8, they’re out of the game.

Jets @ Ravens — A Quiet Climb
The Jets came in as the league’s easy prey,
But Baltimore’s spark felt dim that day.
Still, Lamar guided a steady score,
Nothing flashy, but enough for more.
23-10, the win drives their drive,
At 6-5, they pass us alive.

Browns @ Raiders — A Rookie Dawn
Shadeur Sanders took the field with pride,
His first true start, no place to hide.
He wasn’t perfect, but showed real flare,
Even Garrett watched stunned from the sideline chair.
24-10, a win at last,
At 3-8, hope forms fast.
