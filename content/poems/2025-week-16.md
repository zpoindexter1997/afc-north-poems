Bengals @ Dolphins — Playing for Pride
Cincy went south with nothing to gain,
Still ran the starters, Burrow in the rain.
Against rookie Ewers, it wasn’t a test,
The Bengals poured it on, looked their best.
45 before the fourth could begin,
45-21 — a hollow win.

Bills @ Browns — Nobody’s a Free Win
Buffalo came in chalked as a lock,
But Shadeur fired early, stunned the block.
Back and forth, the Browns stood tall,
Refusing to crumble, refusing to fall.
Garrett hit 22, still chasing Watt’s crown,
23-20 — but respect goes around.

Steelers @ Lions — Survive and Advance
A playoff-feel clash in Detroit’s den,
Steelers struck first and struck again.
The run was erased, the lead held tight,
Warren and Gainwell sparked the fight.
A last-second TD wiped out by OPI lore,
29-24 — we walk out the door.

Patriots @ Ravens — Silence in Baltimore
New England came loud, the chants on repeat,
But Lamar went down early, back injury defeat.
Henry carried weight till the final drive call,
Then he too sat, and the Ravens did fall.
Drake Maye heard MVP echoes all night,
Baltimore went 24-28, losing the fight.

Standings after Week 16:
Steelers 9-6 | Ravens 7-8 | Bengals 5-10 | Browns 3-12
Division Narrative:
Pittsburgh survives chaos and controls its destiny.
Baltimore’s season unravels with injuries at the worst time.
Cincinnati lights up bad teams, but it’s far too late.
Cleveland earns league-wide respect — even in defeat.
