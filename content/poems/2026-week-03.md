AFC North Recap Week 3 - 9/28/2026
"Rivalries, Resilience, and a Race for the North"

Bengals @ Steelers — Anything Can Happen in the North

They counted us out after two weeks of pain,
While Cincy rolled in with confidence to gain.
But records mean nothing when North rivals collide,
And Rodgers found fire with Pittsburgh pride.
Three touchdowns through air, big plays down the field,
While Burrow's protection refused to yield.
Then Cincy's special teams started losing control,
A punter interference gave Pittsburgh life and a goal.
A muffed punt followed, recovered by Steel,
Yet Burrow had two minutes to answer the deal.
Then Pittsburgh broke through — strip sack, ball secured,
30-27 — the North just got thrown into war.

Panthers @ Browns — Cleveland Finds a Way

Carolina came in with the league taking note,
Two weeks of great football had Panthers hopes afloat.
But Cleveland stayed with them, ugly and tight,
Finding just enough to keep winning the fight.
It came to the final drive, everything on the line,
And somehow the Browns pulled it off right on time.
Two straight victories keep their North hopes alive,
And Watson keeps his job as Cleveland continues to thrive.

Ravens vs Cowboys — Seven Seconds in Brazil

Henry kept pounding, his legend still grows,
Third all-time in touchdowns, with no signs he'll slow.
Dallas fought back, tying thirty-one all,
Then seven seconds remained for one final call.
Lamar launched deep, Zay Flowers came through,
One second remained — there was one thing to do.
From fifty-six yards, the walk-off sailed in,
34-31 — Baltimore stuns them for the win.

Standings after Week 3: Steelers 2-1 | Ravens 2-1 | Browns 2-1 | Bengals 2-1

Division Narrative:
Pittsburgh finally finds its offense when it matters most, handing Cincinnati its first loss in a wild AFC North rivalry.
Baltimore needed only seven seconds for Lamar, Flowers, and a 56-yard miracle to steal an unforgettable win in Brazil.
Cleveland has somehow won two straight, and suddenly this team refuses to be dismissed.
Cincinnati falls from first to a four-way tie after special-teams disasters turn a winnable rivalry game into heartbreak.

Three weeks down: four teams, four identical records — the AFC North is officially anybody's war.
