In the heart of the North, where rivalries thrive,
The Steelers stood proud, now four-and-two, alive.
Against the Jets, with headlines all abuzz,
Rodgers and Adams, back together as was,
But Pittsburgh’s defense was fierce, no fear to show,
Blocked a field goal, and picked off throws in tow.
And Russell, who stepped up with nothing to hide,
He led the black and gold with grit, Steelers Nation – “Let’s Ride”.
The Bengals faced the Browns in a contest so tight,
But Burrow’s squad just managed to cling to the fight.
Yet Cleveland’s hopes took a sorrowful turn,
As Watson fell once more, a painful return.
But with Chubb back on the field, there’s still a spark,
For Browns fans’ dreams that linger in the dark.
Now eyes turn to Baltimore, where the Ravens soar,
With MVP Lamar ready for more.
Against Tampa’s Baker, who’s earned his place,
A duel of QBs, each seeking grace.
But if the Ravens falter on this prime-time night,
The Steelers rise back to the AFC North's height.
So here we stand, in this gridiron war,
Where the Steel Curtain seeks to reign once more.
In the land of fierce battles, victory calls,
And Pittsburgh remains, the heart of them all.
