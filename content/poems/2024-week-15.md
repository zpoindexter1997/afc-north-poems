The Steelers vs. Eagles—a hard-fought fray,
Without Pickens, our offense lost its way.
Yet the defense stood tall, a sight to see,
Making Barkley look mortal, just like you or me.
But Hurts found his mark, his arm took flight,
As we clinched playoffs, even in spite.
Still, a shadow looms, a fan's dismay—
TJ Watt’s ankle—please, let him play.
The Bengals met Titans, a first-half stumble,
But their defense rose, making Tennessee crumble.
A pick at the goal line—a touchback’s might,
Turned Mason Rudolph into Levis' night.
Burrow’s Bengals held their ground,
Their playoff hopes continue to sound.
The Browns and Chiefs, a brutal grind,
With Jameis benched, they sought to find
DTR’s spark, a hopeful stride,
But Mahomes’ fall left fans horrified.
A loss for Cleveland, but they left their mark,
A battle fought fiercely in Arrowhead Park.
The Ravens soared against the Giants’ line,
Lamar ran wild—his mama’s sign.
Domination painted in purple hues,
The Giants could only lick their wounds and lose.
Now the North turns its eyes to the prize,
Steelers and Ravens, where sparks will fly.
The division crown is ours to seize,
If we tame their wings and bend their knees.
Steelers Nation stands proud, hearts ablaze,
Ready to light up the AFC North’s stage.
