# AFC North Recap Week 8 - 11/1/2026
*"Revenge, Survival, and a Kingdom in Ruins"*

### Browns @ Steelers — The Steel City Settles the Score

They beat us on Thursday, thought they'd conquered the Steel,  
But Pittsburgh remembered exactly how that loss made us feel.  
The offense wasn't flashy, but Warren brought the fight,  
While the defense made Watson regret showing up that night.  
Sacks came in bunches, the football came loose,  
And Cleveland's whole offense was left without excuse.  
No big plays allowed, no room left to run,  
Four quarters of punishment, and Pittsburgh wasn't done.  
Watson took such a beating, he briefly left the field,  
While Cleveland's hopes of staying first were forced to yield.  
The rematch was ours, and the North's crown was claimed,  
**27-6 — the Steelers put the Dawg Pound to shame.**

---

### Titans @ Bengals — Burrow's Beautiful Disaster

The Titans came in with just one lonely win,  
But nobody told them they couldn't compete with Cincy again.  
Three picks from Burrow, sacks left and right,  
Yet somehow he kept throwing touchdowns all night.  
Five scores, four-sixty-two through the air,  
A masterpiece and a meltdown beyond compare.  
Dexter Lawrence stood tall while Tennessee fought on,  
**43-36 in overtime — the Bengals survived till dawn.**

---

### Ravens @ Bills — The Fall of a Kingdom

Last year these giants gave football a show,  
This year Buffalo dealt Baltimore a crushing blow.  
Lamar came back, but on snap number one,  
A thumb injury meant his comeback was done.  
Surgery and injured reserve — the worst kind of news,  
Then Snoop went down too, leaving nothing to choose.  
Third-string under center, their hopes torn apart,  
**36-10 — the Bills ripped out Baltimore's heart.**

**Division Narrative:**

Pittsburgh takes sole possession of first place after avenging its earlier loss to Cleveland, riding a punishing defense and Jaylen Warren's steady production into control of the North.

Cincinnati survives an overtime thriller against a one-win Tennessee team, with Joe Burrow somehow throwing five touchdowns and three interceptions in the same chaotic performance.

Cleveland's surprising reign comes crashing down in Pittsburgh, as Watson gets battered, the offense disappears, and the Browns surrender the division lead.

Baltimore's nightmare deepens as Lamar Jackson returns only to suffer a thumb injury requiring surgery, Huntley goes down too, and the Ravens fall to .500 with their quarterback situation in ruins.

**Eight weeks down: Pittsburgh takes the throne, Cincinnati keeps swinging, Cleveland gets humbled — and Baltimore's kingdom has lost its king.**
