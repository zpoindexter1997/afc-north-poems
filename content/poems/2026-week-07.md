# AFC North Recap Week 7 - 10/25/2026
*"The North Has a New King"*

### Steelers vs Saints — A Parisian Snoozefest

The streets of Paris were filled with unrest,  
But the NFL pressed on with its international test.  
The crowds came for football, for glory and flair,  
Instead got a punt fest with little to spare.  
Our defense stood strong, giving chances to score,  
But Rodgers and company could barely do more.  
No fireworks in France, just a painfully slow scene,  
**16-10 — Pittsburgh escapes with the win.**

---

### Bengals @ Ravens — The Jungle Invades Baltimore

We rooted for Cincy, a bitter affair,  
But nobody expected the beating they'd share.  
Henry got stuffed, couldn't break through the wall,  
And Snoop looked lost whenever he'd throw the ball.  
Burrow found Chase, and Tee joined the show,  
While Baltimore's offense had nowhere to go.  
A rivalry massacre, the Ravens looked done,  
**35-13 — the Bengals had all of the fun.**

---

### Browns @ Titans — Cleveland Won't Go Away

They said it was luck, that the Browns couldn't last,  
But Cleveland keeps winning and silencing the past.  
Tennessee fought hard, kept the game within reach,  
Yet Watson and company had lessons to teach.  
The fans want him gone, no new contract in sight,  
But somehow he's leading this team through the fight.  
A three-point escape, and the North's theirs to claim,  
**27-24 — Cleveland's still running the game.**

**Division Narrative:**

Cleveland sits atop the AFC North with a 5-2 record and head-to-head victories over Pittsburgh and Baltimore, proving that its surprising season is becoming impossible to ignore.

Pittsburgh has quietly won three straight, though a painfully sluggish performance in Paris raises questions about whether the offense can keep up with the division's best.

Cincinnati makes the loudest statement of the week, demolishing Baltimore behind Burrow, Chase, and Higgins while keeping itself firmly in the division race.

Baltimore has gone from division leader to last place in just two weeks, and Lamar Jackson's absence is turning a promising season into a growing crisis.

**Seven weeks down: Cleveland wears the crown, Pittsburgh lurks behind, Cincinnati roars, and Baltimore's kingdom is burning to the ground.**
