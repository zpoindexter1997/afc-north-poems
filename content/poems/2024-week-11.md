In the land where the steel meets the sky,
Where the Black and Gold rise, and the Ravens fly,
We watched the chaos, the drama unfold,
While the Browns and Bengals did what we’ve been told—
That football’s a game of more than just flair,
It’s about grit, and defense, and sometimes despair.
The Browns, they stumbled, their kicker was lost,
Three field goals missed, at such a high cost.
Taysom Hill danced, a Swiss Army knife,
Jameis still struggling, “No W's” in sight.
Saints left them wounded, a sad, hopeless tale,
Their future uncertain, their season for sale.
Then came the Bengals, down 6 to 27,
A team in a hole, but their spirit was heaven.
They battled back, with heart and with soul,
Came close to the win, but they lost control.
Two missed field goals—what a kicker’s curse!
A touchdown from Chargers, for better or worse.
But oh, how we Steelers love to see
That in the AFC North, there’s one clear decree:
It’s not about flash or the showiest plays,
It’s about tough defense and Boswell’s gaze.
Steelers vs Ravens—what a sight,
Diontae on the other side, feeling the bite.
He dropped every ball, we all saw the truth,
That’s why we let him go—no need to recoup.
Then Patrick Queen, he brought the heat,
With a hit and a sack, it couldn’t be beat.
A touchdown was missing, but defense stood tall,
With Justin Tucker’s misses, we answered the call.
Six field goals, Chris Boswell’s the king,
He put us on his back, made the stadium sing.
16-18, the Ravens left reeling,
While Steelers fans bask in the sweet, steady feeling.
8-2, we’re kings of the North,
All credit to defense and kickers who’re worth
Every penny, every ounce of praise,
Steelers reign supreme, we own these days.
So here’s to our future, bright as can be,
For in Pittsburgh, we know that defense wins the sea.
We don’t need no flash, we just need the grind—
And in the AFC North, the Steelers are prime!
