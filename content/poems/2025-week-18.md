Ravens @ Steelers — Wide Right, Write History
Sunday night thunder, the North on the line,
No Metcalf, no Washington — the odds misaligned.
Lamar was back, the Ravens came hot,
First half purple, everything we feared we got.
But steel doesn’t fold, it bends and replies,
Second-half grit lit fire in our eyes.
Big plays conceded, the tension ran tight,
Until one last kick decided the night.
Loop lined it up, the stadium froze—
Wide right sailed hope. 26–24, North is ours in gold.

Browns @ Bengals — Records in Ruin
Cleveland came south with chaos in tow,
Cincy still searching for something to show.
Burrow battled hard, the tempers all flared,
Every snap tense, every whistle stared.
Then Garrett broke through, history secured,
Twenty-three sacks — a record ensured.
The moment derailed all rhythm and fate,
Browns escaped late, 20–18 sealed it straight.
Two seasons end bruised, but not the same tale—
One writes records, the other sits 6–11, stale.

Final Division Narrative:
Pittsburgh survives the storm — doubted, battered, but unbroken — kings of the North.
Baltimore falls inches short, a season haunted by injuries and timing.
Cleveland finds pride in disruption and history, not wins.
Cincinnati ends with talent intact but identity fractured, questions louder than answers.

Steelers 2025 Legacy Poem — “Steel City Resurrection”
Steel City rose up from Week 1’s uncertain spark,

Warren and Gainwell danced through gaps — old school, new loud.

Steel City resurrected, the North once again secured —
10–7, AFC North champs, destiny ensured.
