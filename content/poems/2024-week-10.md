In the North, where the gridiron’s fierce and bold,
Legends are forged, and rivalries grow old.
The Bengals and Ravens, a clash for the ages,
Lamar and Burrow, filling history’s pages.
Burrow slings it, heart on his sleeve,
Chase in stride—too slick to believe.
But fate, that fickle ref in the sky,
Left Burrow on the turf, no flag to fly.
A sneaky facemask on the game’s last breath,
Ravens snatched the win from the jaws of death.
Meanwhile, the Steelers were primed for a test,
Against the Commanders, the so-called “best.”
Rookie Daniels, swift and sharp,
Met the Steel Curtain’s iron harp.
Russ threw deep, a spiral so tight,
Pickens leaped with all his might.
Then to Mike Williams, a bomb, a show,
A dagger to claim the win, and down they go.
Now, we’re top of the North, kings of the fight,
As the Ravens loom under the prime-time light.
Steel against steel, grit upon grit,
This is our house, and we’re ready for it.
