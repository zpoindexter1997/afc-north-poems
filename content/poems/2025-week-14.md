Titans @ Browns — The Rookie Duel
Shadeur vs Cam, draft night déjà vu,
Two rising stars the scouts all knew.
Sanders went wild, a four-TD spree,
But Tennessee’s ground game ran free.
A late two-point try pulled him aside,
31-29 — and Cleveland’s ride died.

Bengals @ Bills — From Glory to Gasp
Burrow and Allen, marquee on display,
Cincy took charge nearly all day.
Allen responded with magic and might,
A forty-yard scramble igniting the fight.
Two picks in the fourth sealed Bengal fate,
39-34 — their hopes deflate.

Steelers @ Ravens — Rodgers Returns Fire

First throw deep, Metcalf prepared.
Bombs kept flying, DK supreme,
One-fifty yards, the spark of the team.
Likely’s TD ruled out — a controversial sting,
27-22 — and Steelers reign king.
