The Terrible Towel waved once more,
As black and gold walked off ahead on the score.
Nobody's pretty, nobody's clean,
But a win is a win, whatever it means.

Meanwhile in Cleveland the Dawg Pound howled,
In Cincinnati the Jungle prowled,
And Baltimore's ravens circled the sky,
Waiting for someone else's team to die.

Four weeks from now, or five, or ten,
This whole division flips again.
For now the Towel gets one more wave —
Steel City lives to fight, and rave.
