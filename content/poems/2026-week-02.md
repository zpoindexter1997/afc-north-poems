AFC North Recap Week 2 - 9/20/2026

Steelers @ Patriots — A Decade on Repeat

The defense made chances, but nothing would spark,
While Pittsburgh's offense stayed painfully dark.
Three-and-outs mounted, DK couldn't receive,
Another wasted effort the defense can't believe.
Rodgers looked ancient, McCarthy brought nothing new,
Just the same stale offense we've suffered through.
No touchdowns, no answers, nothing to see,
20-3 — Pittsburgh falls to a painful 1-1 misery.

Bengals @ Texans — Burrow and Chase Take Flight

Cincinnati hit Houston and controlled from the start,
That suddenly fierce defense again played its part.
The Texans found nothing, their chances stayed few,
While Burrow kept dealing like we knew he could do.
Then Chase broke deep and the football took flight,
A touchdown connection for the highlight of the night.
The Bengals keep rolling, their hot start lives on,
20-6 — Cincy sits alone at 2-0, strong.

Saints @ Ravens — The Giants Look Mortal

Baltimore came roaring, as dangerous as claimed,
Then New Orleans answered and completely changed the game.
Lamar found resistance, King Henry did too,
The unstoppable Ravens struggled to break through.
The Saints kept fighting and stole it away,
A late touchdown dagger decided the day.
With Flowers still missing, Baltimore came undone,
24-17 — the Ravens stumble back to 1-1.

Browns @ Buccaneers — Revenge Denied

Baker saw Cleveland with revenge on his mind,
Facing the old team that once left him behind.
But Cleveland's defense refused to let him ignite,
While even their offense surprisingly looked right.
The skies opened up and put football on hold,
But after the storm, Cleveland wouldn't fold.
Baker's revenge spoiled when the final bell rung,
23-19 — the Browns shock Tampa and move to 1-1.

Standings after Week 2: Bengals 2-0 | Steelers 1-1 | Ravens 1-1 | Browns 1-1

Division Narrative:
Cincinnati owns the North outright, with Burrow-to-Chase thriving and a defense that's suddenly terrifying.
Pittsburgh's defense deserves hazard pay for having to watch its own offense play football.
Baltimore finally bleeds, reminding the division that Lamar and Henry can be contained.
Cleveland delivers the surprise of the week, spoiling Baker's revenge before Watson heads toward a homecoming that could get very loud.

Two weeks down: Cincinnati takes the throne, Cleveland finds life, Baltimore looks mortal — and Pittsburgh looks painfully familiar.
