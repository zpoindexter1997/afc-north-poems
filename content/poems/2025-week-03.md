Steelers Steel the Ball
The offense stalled, no sparks in sight,
But the defense roared, a dominant night.
One pick, four fumbles, all brought home,
A wall of steel where Patriots roam.
21-14, the grind was done,
At 2-1, the fight’s just begun.

Bengals Browning Blues
With Burrow out, the hope looked thin,
Browning stepped up but couldn’t win.
Fumbles and failures, the Vikings struck fast,
A 48-10 loss, a brutal contrast.
At 2-1, the doubts run deep,
Without their star, can Cincy keep?

Cleveland’s Shock the Pack
Green Bay stormed in with title dreams,
But Cleveland broke those shining schemes.
The Browns hung close, refused to fall,
A late-game push surprised them all.
13-10, the upset made,
At 1-2, a spark displayed.

Ravens Await the Lions
Prime time calls, the stage is set,
Two titans clashing, a night to sweat.
If Baltimore wins, they rise above,
With Henry’s power and Flowers’ glove.
The North will watch with sharpened eyes,
Will Ravens climb, or face surprise?
