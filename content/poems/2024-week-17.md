On Christmas morn, we braved the cold,
The Steelers’ fight, our hearts consoled.
But oh, the Chiefs brought grief untold,
As hopes of victory turned to fool's gold.
The defense left, where were they now?
A shadow of their famed Steel plow.
Pointing fingers, casting blame,
This wasn’t our Pittsburgh’s game.
Across the North, the Ravens soared,
Their dominance once more restored.
Lamar, electric, stole the show,
MVP buzz began to grow.
On Saturday, the Bengals fought,
A playoff dream, their only thought.
Bo Nix dazzled, yet Denver pressed,
OT drama, hearts distressed.
The Bengals won, their path alive,
The Broncos’ hopes could not survive.
Our task grows grim, the battle clear,
To stop their playoff push right here.
And Cleveland’s clash, a one-sided tale,
The Dolphins danced; the Browns turned pale.
No need to linger, the story’s done,
A stomp so hard, it blocked the sun.
Next week we march, our hearts still steel,
To fight the Bengals with grit and zeal.
Though Christmas crushed us, a grim prelude,
We’ll rise to battle, in black and gold renewed.
