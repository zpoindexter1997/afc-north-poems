Bengals @ Ravens — Burrow Breaks the Balance
Thanksgiving night with the North on the line,
Burrow returned right on perfect time.
Five turnovers forced, the defense went feral,
Baltimore rattled from start to barrel.
32-14, Lamar looked untrue,
At 4-8, the Bengals shocked the view.

49ers @ Browns — Welcome to Reality
Shadeur’s second start met red and gold,
San Fran ran wild, relentless and bold.
Every phase cracked, no answers found,
The Browns just sank into the ground.
26-8, the lesson hit late—
At 3-9, they’re stuck with their fate.

Bills @ Steelers — A Night to Forget
Buffalo walked into Pittsburgh loud,
We jumped ahead, the crowd was proud.
Then Rodgers got crushed, a scoop-and-score scar,
Blood on his face, fractured wrist war.
Rudolph threw one pick, Rodgers came back hurt—
26-7, and the season feels cursed.
