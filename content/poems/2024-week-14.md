Oh, the AFC North, where battles are fierce,
A division of grit, where rivalries pierce.
From the Steel City hills to Baltimore's bay,
To Ohio's two fronts, each Sunday’s a fray.
Last week, in Cleveland, a rematch took stage,
The Steelers, relentless, unleashed all their rage.
With no Pickens in sight, Russ led the attack,
Cooking up magic and never looking back.
A 27-14 triumph sealed their fate,
The Browns' playoff hopes met a bitter slate.
The Ravens were resting, their Bye came in stride,
But no rest will save them from what’s deep inside.
Their flight may be mighty, their records may gleam,
Yet cracks in their armor shadow the team.
The Bengals, on Monday, brought cartoons to life,
Joe Burrow as Bart, in this playoff strife.
With Chase as Lisa, they sparked up the field,
A touchdown finale, their hope’s not yet sealed.
Next week, the Eagles await in their nest,
A challenge, no doubt, but we’ll rise to the test.
For Pittsburgh’s not fading; our roar’s growing loud,
When we beat them, we’ll silence the doubt in the crowd.
