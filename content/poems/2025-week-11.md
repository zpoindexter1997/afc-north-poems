Bengals @ Steelers — Revenge Delivered in Full
Revenge night at Acrisure, the air running hot,
This time the defense showed everything they’ve got.
Rodgers was cooking ’til his wrist gave way,
Then Mason Rudolph stepped in to steady the day.
He kept things moving, calm in his stride,
While the defense erupted with Pittsburgh pride.
A pick-six sparked life, a runaway flame,
The fumble return touchdown finished the game.
Chase got locked down, frustration grew,
Spitting on Ramsey — the lowest of moves.
Ramsey swung back, and out he went,
34-12 — our message was sent.
Steelers 6-4. Bengals 3-7. Playoff dreams? Bent.

Ravens @ Browns — A New QB Dawn?
Cleveland’s defense came roaring out strong,
Holding Lamar down for a quarter-long song.
The game stayed close, a battle of wills,
Tension thick like wind on the hills.
Then Gabriel went down, the crowd filled with fear,
Shadeur Sanders trotted in for his first real career.
He showed raw flashes, a spark in debris,
But 4-for-16 tells the truth honestly.
An interception hurt, the timing was rough,
But the kid’s got something — maybe enough.
Baltimore pulled away, 23-16,
Ravens 5-5. Browns 2-8 — hope still unseen.

Standings after Week 11:
Steelers 6-4 (still leading the North), Ravens 5-5, Bengals 3-7, Browns 2-8
Division Narrative:
Pittsburgh reclaims the North with authority, fueled by defense and bad blood.
Baltimore stays in striking distance, steady and dangerous.
Cincinnati collapses beneath chaos, discipline gone and dreams fading.
Cleveland may have finally found their future — but their present is rough.
