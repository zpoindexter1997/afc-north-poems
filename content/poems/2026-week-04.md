AFC North Recap Week 4 - 10/5/2026
"Thursday Night Terror and a New King in the North"

Steelers @ Browns — Cleveland Owns Thursday Night

We came into Cleveland expecting a win,
But Thursday night brought that nightmare again.
Eight straight TNF victories now in their hand,
The longest active streak — somehow Cleveland's brand.
Watson looked sharper than we've seen him in years,
While rookie receivers gave Pittsburgh new fears.
The Browns jumped ahead and controlled from the start,
But Pittsburgh fought back and showed a little heart.
We clawed within three with a chance still alive,
Yet never found enough for that final drive.
A rivalry reminder: throw records out of sight,
27-24 — Cleveland owns Thursday night.

Jaguars @ Bengals — Flagged, Beaten, and Baffled

Jacksonville came to Cincy and couldn't stay clean,
Flag after flag — one of the sloppiest halves seen.
Ten-plus penalties, over a hundred yards burned,
Yet somehow the Bengals were the ones getting churned.
Cincy stayed close, but the Jags stayed ahead,
Then Chase left injured, adding more dread.
All those mistakes and Jacksonville still prevailed,
22-17 — Cincinnati somehow failed.

Titans @ Ravens — Revenge Takes a Back Seat

Henry saw Tennessee, his old team in town,
But this revenge game wasn't where he wore the crown.
The Titans contained him, never letting him feast,
So Lamar and Flowers simply unleashed.
For moments Tennessee threatened to steal it away,
But Baltimore's stars had too much on display.
Then Lamar hurt his ankle, clouding the win,
24-18 — Ravens rise, but concern settles in.

Standings after Week 4: Ravens 3-1 | Browns 3-1 | Steelers 2-2 | Bengals 2-2

Division Narrative:
Baltimore takes its place atop the North, but Lamar's injury could completely change what comes next.
Cleveland has gone from division punchline to 3-1 contender, riding an eight-game Thursday night winning streak and suddenly dangerous offense.
Pittsburgh finally showed offensive life late, but getting bullied by Cleveland after entering as favorites makes this one hurt.
Cincinnati drops its second straight, somehow losing to a Jaguars team that spent half the afternoon beating itself.

Four weeks down: Cleveland is somehow thriving, Pittsburgh and Cincinnati are stumbling — and Baltimore has the throne, but may have lost its king.
