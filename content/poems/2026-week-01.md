Four teams, one basement, endless spite,
A division built on cold and fright.
The Ravens caw, the Bengals prowl,
The Browns dig deep, the Steelers scowl.

Week one is here, the tape is clean,
Everybody's undefeated, calm, serene.
Ask me again in eight or nine,
When someone's coach is on the line.

So welcome back to this fine tradition,
A weekly poem, a modest mission:
Recap the chaos, praise the pain,
And do it all again next Monday (or Tuesday, if MNF stains the week).
