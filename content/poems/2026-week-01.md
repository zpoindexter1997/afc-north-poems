AFC North Recap Week 1 — 9/14/2026

A new season, a clean slate — and the North wastes absolutely no time getting weird.

Falcons @ Steelers — Defense Sets the Standard

Atlanta came to Pittsburgh with Rush at command,
Thirty carries for Bijan, their whole battle plan.
Two picks from the defense, no passing lanes in sight,
While our offense moved slowly, still searching for its bite.
The pieces are there — now find a rhythm and run,
20-13, Pittsburgh starts the season 1-0.

Buccaneers @ Bengals — Defense in Cincinnati?!

A shootout was promised when Tampa came to town,
Instead Cincy’s defense kept dragging Baker down.
Four fumbles forced, even one at the goal,
While Burrow’s pick-six briefly dug them a hole.
The defense won Cincy a game — yeah, you read that right,
33-27, the Bengals start 1-0 tonight.

Ravens @ Colts — Same Old Monster

Baltimore hit Indy and immediately took flight,
Henry laughed at Father Time and ran with all his might.
Lamar looked electric, that MVP flame still burns,
While another Ravens offense has the whole league concerned.
Forty-one points later, Super Bowl whispers begun,
41-23, Baltimore starts the year 1-0.

Browns @ Jaguars — Watson Wears Out His Welcome

Cleveland chose Watson, to everybody’s dismay,
And Jacksonville quickly made them regret it that day.
The boos have grown louder, his play gave them more,
As another dreadful showing left Cleveland wanting the door.
The calls for the bench may have already begun,
34-10, and the Browns stumble to 0-1.

Standings after Week 1: Steelers 1-0 | Ravens 1-0 | Bengals 1-0 | Browns 0-1

Division Narrative:
Pittsburgh starts with a win and a defense ready to feast, but that offense needs to wake up fast.
Cincinnati somehow wins because of its defense — apparently we're starting the season in an alternate universe.
Baltimore already looks terrifying, with Lamar and Henry picking up exactly where they left off.
And Cleveland? One week in, and the Watson experiment already looks like something everyone wants ended.

The North is back. Three teams strike first. Cleveland strikes out.
